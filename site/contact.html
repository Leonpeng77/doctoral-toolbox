Contact

Report an error or suggest a rule

Corrections are welcome. The most useful report includes the input you used, the output you received, and the rule or source you believe should apply.

Email

qpengking@gmail.com

There is no contact form on this site because that would require sending your message through a third-party form service. A plain email keeps the site dependency-free.

What helps

  • The name of the tool and the source type selected.
  • A short, synthetic example that reproduces the problem.
  • The output you expected, and the manual section, institutional template or journal guideline that supports it.
  • For the auditor, the exact line that was flagged correctly or incorrectly.

What to leave out

Please do not send unpublished manuscript text, supervisor feedback, participant data, or anything covered by an ethics approval or a confidentiality agreement. A made-up example with the same structure is always enough to diagnose a formatting rule. This site has no server-side storage, but email is not an appropriate channel for research data.

What this address cannot help with

We cannot check a whole reference list for you, advise on a specific university's template, write or edit academic work, or provide an official interpretation of APA style. For an authoritative answer, consult the Publication Manual (7th ed.), your graduate school's formatting guide, or your target journal's author instructions. For questions about how the site handles data, see the privacy policy.
