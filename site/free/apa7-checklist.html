What's in it

Seven groups, ordered by when you should do them: [7d] while the draft is still open, [24h] once the text is locked, [1h] immediately before upload. Here are twelve of the 67 checks so you can see whether it is worth asking for.

A. In-text citations [7d]

  1. Every in-text citation has a matching reference-list entry.
  2. Three or more authors use et al. from the first citation.
  3. Direct quotations include a page number, paragraph number or timestamp.

B. Reference list structure [7d]

  1. A 0.5-inch hanging indent is applied to every entry, not typed with spaces.
  2. All punctuation is half-width ASCII, not full-width ，。（）.
  3. No entry ends with a period after a DOI or URL.

C. Reference list content [7d]

  1. Corporate authors are spelled out in full, not inverted into initials.
  2. Journal names are in Title Case and italicised, together with the volume number.

G. File and submission [1h]

  1. Fonts are embedded; no missing-glyph boxes appear in the PDF.
  2. Click every DOI and hyperlink in the final PDF — they must resolve.
  3. File name follows the required convention, with no final_v2 version words.

A note on what this is

It is a formatting memory aid, not a substitute for the Publication Manual of the American Psychological Association (7th ed.), your institution's dissertation template or your journal's author guidelines.
