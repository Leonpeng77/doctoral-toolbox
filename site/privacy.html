Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: the tools run in your browser. We do not receive, store or analyse anything you type into them.

Last updated: 10 November 2026. If the site's features change, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

1. Who runs this site

Doctoral Toolbox is an independent educational project. Contact details are on the contact page. For the purposes of data protection law, the site maintainer is the controller of any personal data processed through the site.

2. Data you type into the tools

The APA 7 reference builder and the reference list auditor are JavaScript programs that execute locally in your browser. The text you enter is used only to compute the result shown on your own screen. It is not submitted to a server, not written to a cookie, not saved in local or session storage, and not logged. Reloading the page discards it. There is no account system and no sign-up.

This includes the "Smart import" feature on the reference checker page: its parser is another local JavaScript file using deterministic rules. A companion cloud API exists as a separate, non-commercial development prototype, but this website does not call it and no text entered here is transmitted to Azure or to any other service.

Because no data leaves your device, no automated decision-making, profiling or AI processing is performed on the content of your manuscript.

3. Data collected by hosting

The site is served as static files through a content delivery network. Like any web host, that provider may process technical information needed to deliver a page, such as your IP address, browser type, requested URL and timestamp, for security and operational purposes. This is governed by the hosting provider's own privacy notice. We do not receive a per-visitor analytics profile from the site at launch, and we do not run third-party analytics scripts.

4. Cookies at launch

At launch the site sets no cookies of its own: no analytics cookies, no advertising cookies, no preference cookies. The tools do not need them.

5. If advertising is added later

The site may introduce display advertising to cover domain and hosting costs. If that happens, third-party advertising providers — typically Google and its advertising partners — may set cookies or use similar technologies to serve ads, measure them, and limit how often you see the same ad. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Where required by the GDPR, the UK GDPR or similar law, a consent mechanism will be presented to visitors in those regions before non-essential cookies are set.
  • Advertising will not be placed inside a tool's input or output area, and adding advertising will not change what the tools do with your text: nothing is transmitted.
  • California residents may exercise rights under the CCPA/CPRA by using the contact details on this site. We do not sell personal information.

6. External links

The site links to external resources such as style guidance and privacy opt-out pages. Once you follow an external link, that site's own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for the content or practices of third-party sites.

7. Children

This site is written for doctoral researchers and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of personal data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As the site collects no account data and no content data, the fastest route for any question is the contact page.

9. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Introducing analytics or advertising will always be accompanied by an update to this policy before those features go live.
