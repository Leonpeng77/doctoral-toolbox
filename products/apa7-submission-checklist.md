# The APA 7 Submission Checklist

**For doctoral dissertations, theses and journal manuscripts**
Version 1.0 · November 2026 · Free to use and share with attribution to Doctoral Toolbox

Work through the list in order. The three timing labels are suggestions:
**[7d]** do while the draft is still open · **[24h]** do after the text is locked · **[1h]** do immediately before upload.

> This checklist covers formatting, not argument. It is a companion to the APA 7 manual, your institution's dissertation template, and your target journal's author guidelines. Where they conflict, the institution or journal wins.

---

## A. In-text citations · [7d]

- [ ] Every claim that is not common knowledge has a citation.
- [ ] Every in-text citation has a matching reference-list entry.
- [ ] Every reference-list entry is cited somewhere in the text.
- [ ] Two-author citations use `&` in parentheses and `and` in narrative form.
- [ ] Three or more authors use `et al.` from the **first** citation (APA 7).
- [ ] Group authors are spelled out on first citation; the abbreviation is introduced correctly.
- [ ] Direct quotations include a page number, paragraph number or timestamp.
- [ ] Quotations of 40 words or more are set as an indented block quote without quotation marks.
- [ ] Same-author-same-year works are disambiguated as `2020a`, `2020b` in both places.
- [ ] Personal communications are cited in text only, never in the reference list.
- [ ] Multiple works inside one set of parentheses are ordered alphabetically, separated by semicolons.

## B. Reference list structure · [7d]

- [ ] Entries are alphabetised letter by letter by the first author's surname.
- [ ] The list is double-spaced throughout, with no extra blank lines between entries.
- [ ] A 0.5-inch hanging indent is applied to every entry (not manually typed spaces).
- [ ] The heading is **References**, bold and centred (or per your template).
- [ ] One space only after every punctuation mark; no double spaces anywhere.
- [ ] All punctuation is half-width ASCII, not full-width `，。；（）`.
- [ ] No entry ends with a period after a DOI or URL.

## C. Reference list content · [7d]

- [ ] Author surnames and initials are correct; initials are separated by spaces (`Smith, J. A.`).
- [ ] Corporate authors (`American Psychological Association`) are spelled out in full, not inverted.
- [ ] Two authors are joined with `, & `; three to twenty use a serial comma before `&`.
- [ ] For 21+ authors: first 19, an ellipsis, then the final author, with no ampersand.
- [ ] `et al.` does **not** appear anywhere in the reference list.
- [ ] The year is in parentheses, or `(n.d.)` when genuinely undated.
- [ ] Article, book and chapter titles are in **sentence case**.
- [ ] Journal names are in Title Case and italicised, together with the volume number.
- [ ] The issue number in parentheses is **not** italicised.
- [ ] Journal page ranges have no `pp.`; chapter page ranges do.
- [ ] Book titles, dissertation titles and website names are italicised as appropriate.
- [ ] No `Vol.`, `Volume`, `Retrieved from`, `Available from` or `Accessed on`.
- [ ] Publisher location is omitted (APA 7 change); edition statements are kept.
- [ ] Every DOI is written as `https://doi.org/10.xxxx/...` with no label.
- [ ] Articles without a DOI that came from an academic database end at the page range, with no URL.
- [ ] Bracketed descriptions (`[Doctoral dissertation]`, `[Data set]`) are present where the type is not obvious.

## D. Tables and figures · [24h]

- [ ] Each table has a bold number and an italic title on separate lines.
- [ ] Each figure has a bold number, italic title, and the image below.
- [ ] Every table and figure is called out in the text before it appears.
- [ ] Notes (general, specific, probability) appear below the table in that order.
- [ ] Copyright attribution is present for reproduced or adapted material.
- [ ] Column headings are sentence case; units are stated in the heading, not each cell.
- [ ] Decimal values are consistently rounded; leading zeros are omitted where the statistic cannot exceed 1.

## E. Manuscript formatting · [24h]

- [ ] Margins, font, font size and line spacing match the template exactly.
- [ ] Heading levels follow APA 7 (centred bold level 1, flush-left bold level 2, and so on).
- [ ] Page numbers are in the header, top right, starting on the title page.
- [ ] Running head (if required by the journal) is present and short.
- [ ] Title page, abstract, keywords, body, references, appendices and tables/figures are in the required order.
- [ ] The abstract word count is within the journal's limit; keywords are present if required.
- [ ] Appendix labels (`Appendix A`) and their in-text callouts match.
- [ ] No widowed headings, orphaned lines or stray page breaks.
- [ ] Headings and captions are not split across pages.

## F. Language and ethics · [7d]

- [ ] No first-person ambiguity; voice is consistent throughout.
- [ ] Bias-free language per APA 7 (person-first or identity-first as appropriate).
- [ ] Abbreviations are defined on first use; acronyms are not overused.
- [ ] All co-authors, ethics approval numbers, funding and conflict-of-interest statements are present.
- [ ] Data availability and reproducibility statements match the journal's policy.
- [ ] AI tool use is disclosed where the journal or institution requires it.
- [ ] No unpublished manuscript text, supervisor comments or participant data has been sent to third-party services.

## G. File and submission · [1h]

- [ ] The PDF opens on at least two devices and one other PDF viewer.
- [ ] Fonts are embedded; no missing-glyph boxes appear.
- [ ] All hyperlinks and DOIs resolve; click each one in the final PDF.
- [ ] Bookmarks / PDF outline exist for chapter headings, if required.
- [ ] The file is not password-protected or locked against commenting.
- [ ] File name follows the required convention (no spaces, no version words like `final_v2`).
- [ ] Metadata (title, author) does not contain draft notes or previous titles.
- [ ] The correct file is uploaded to the correct submission stage.
- [ ] A timestamped copy of the submitted file is saved outside the working folder.
- [ ] The confirmation email or submission receipt is archived.

## Fast path when you have one hour

If the text is locked and time is short, do **G** first, then **B**, then the DOI and sentence-case items in **C**. Those are the defects examiners and copy-editors notice first, and the ones automated checks miss least often.

Run the free [reference list auditor](https://doctoral-toolbox.app.workbuddy.host/tools/reference-check.html) for the mechanical half, then check italics, hanging indents and cross-references by eye.
